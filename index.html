Read the privacy policy ↗
01

Encrypted at rest.

Your saved credential vault uses AES-256-GCM with a password-derived key. It locks after 30 minutes without explicit use.

02

Codes that stay close.

Cached codes are generated locally, even offline. Google handles sign-in and sync; there's no developer-operated credential server.

03

Access you decide.

Grant website access when linking a site. Code filling happens after you choose an account. No advertising or analytics.
